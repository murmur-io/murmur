## Individual CLA

By signing, **You** (the individual, or the entity You represent) agree:

1. **Definitions.** "Contribution" means any original work of authorship — including modifications or additions — that You intentionally submit to Murmur (via pull request, patch, or otherwise) for inclusion. "Submit" means any form of electronic or written communication sent to the Maintainer or the project, excluding communication conspicuously marked "Not a Contribution."

2. **Copyright license grant.** You grant the Maintainer, and recipients of software distributed by the Maintainer, a **perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license** to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display and perform, sublicense, and **distribute Your Contributions and such derivative works under any license terms, including both AGPL-3.0 and proprietary/commercial terms** (the relicensing grant that enables dual licensing). You retain all right, title, and interest in Your Contributions and may use them for any other purpose.

3. **Patent license grant.** You grant the Maintainer and recipients a **perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable** (except as stated below) patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer Your Contributions, where such license applies only to patent claims licensable by You that are necessarily infringed by Your Contribution alone or by combination with the project. If any entity institutes patent litigation alleging that the project or a Contribution infringes a patent, the patent licenses granted to that entity under this CLA terminate.

4. **You represent that:** each Contribution is Your original creation (or You have the right to submit it under this CLA); You are legally entitled to grant the above licenses; and if Your employer has rights to IP You create, You have received permission to submit on behalf of that employer, or the employer has waived such rights, or the employer has executed a **Corporate CLA** (below).

5. **No obligation / no warranty.** You are not expected to provide support for Your Contributions. Contributions are provided **"AS IS"**, without warranties or conditions of any kind, except as required by law or agreed in writing.

6. **Notice of inaccuracy.** You agree to notify the Maintainer if any statement above becomes inaccurate.

## Corporate CLA (when contributing on behalf of an employer)

If contributions are made in the course of employment, the **employer** signs a Corporate CLA making the same grants as §§2–4 on behalf of the employees it designates, and lists the authorized employee contributors. (A separate `CCLA` text will accompany this once the entity is fixed; it mirrors the individual grants at the organization level.)
