# Dream: Sound & Shape — your meeting as something you can SEE and HEAR

Wave 1 (`2026-07-19-off-the-record.md`) all slid into one heavy register — *witness / defense /
receipts / negotiation*. The user rejected it and asked to be **surprised**, full self-steer. So wave 2
threw out that whole corridor and ran four DELIBERATELY-ORTHOGONAL lenses in parallel — **Murmur as a
creative instrument** · **Murmur when you're alone (intimate/warm)** · **far cross-domain raid**
(gardening/cartography/fermentation/weaving/postal — banned the games/espionage/finance worlds wave 1
used) · **form, space, the senses, wonder**. ~60 fresh ideas, none in the defensive register. Four new
ridges emerged; the one that most literally INVERTS what a notetaker is won the prototype.

## The spark  (survivors — one line each)

1. **Soundprint** *(the winner — prototyped)* — What if every meeting rendered a **glyph you can SEE**:
   a prosody terrain woven from the raw waveform — pitch = ridgeline, loudness = thickness, pauses =
   valleys, laughter = bright bursts, the argument = fast overlapping spikes, You vs Others as two
   braided threads (far-side, no bot) — an "album cover for the conversation" rendered into your vault
   as an owned `.svg`. Click any second to step back into it; a **Pause Garden** collects the loaded
   silences a transcript renders as nothing. *Murmur stops showing you words ABOUT the meeting and
   starts showing you its sound & shape.*
2. **Echo Locate** *(search that answers in the real voice)* — What if searching the Brain returned
   **sound, not text**: type "when we decided to pivot" and hear the actual half-second where the voice
   lifted on "let's do it" — audio-memory chips ranked by prosodic emphasis, not keyword. (Rides the
   already-shipped claim→audio-second anchoring from Receipts.)
3. **The Mother / Sourdough Starter** *(the note that's alive)* — What if a folder kept one living
   **starter note** per project that you feed a spoonful of every meeting; it ferments continuously
   in the background, develops a distinct character over months, goes dormant if you neglect it, rises
   if you feed it — an artifact with real age and lineage, not a re-generated cloud summary. *"Wait —
   my notes are alive?"*
4. **A Letter I Can Read in Five Years** *(intimacy / time)* — What if you record a spoken message to
   future-you, lock it to a date, and on that morning your Mac quietly surfaces past-you's actual
   voice, on schedule — a time capsule no company outage can ever eat, because it's a local file on
   your disk behind Touch ID.

## Why these survived  (atom + emotion)

- **Soundprint** — the purest INVERSION of "here's a summary": it renders the data a transcript throws
  away (prosody, laughter, the held breath) from the raw waveform, with **You/Others as two threads only
  far-side capture (no bot) can separate**, into an **owned `.svg`**. Emotion: **beauty + wonder** — a
  conversation you'd frame. Most screenshot-worthy, most obviously un-cloudable.
- **Echo Locate** — reframes the whole Brain from a text index into a **sonic memory**; the single most
  magical everyday interaction. Stands on VOICE-as-sound + on-device waveform indexing over one store.
- **The Mother** — a genuinely new *object*: a continuously-fermenting local artifact with age and
  lineage, impossible where there's a meter running. Emotion: **tending something alive**.
- **A Letter in Five Years** — LOCK time-gates it, VOICE makes it *you*, OWNED FILES means no company
  can swallow it. Emotion: **tenderness** — a capsule that outlives the service.

## Prototype(s)

`docs/dreams/prototypes/soundprint/index.html` — self-contained HTML+SVG+vanilla-JS, Murmur tokens, no
build. A 38-min **Design Review — Mobile Redesign** rendered as a living prosody terrain: You (cool,
top) and Others (warm amber, bottom) braided over a baseline; a red overlapping spike is the argument
(16:40), a bright burst is laughter (22:20), a dark gap with "···" is the loaded pause (26:00), a mint
dot is the agreement. A white **playhead** scrubs ("Play the shape"); **click any second** → a readout
names the speaker + the line + the tone (and, as progressive enhancement, *speaks it aloud* via
SpeechSynthesis — silent in headless, audible in a real browser). A **Pause Garden** lists the loaded
silences ("4.2s before she said yes"), each jumping the playhead there. A "Shape of the hour" sidebar
reads talk-balance/warmth/tension/resolution off the waveform. Fakes: the ASR, the prosody (procedural),
the audio. Screenshots in `./shots/` (01 full landscape · 02 clicking the pause → the settled "yes").

## What it'd really take  (real seams + honest sizing + limits)

- **Backend (Rust, M):** a prosody-extraction pass over the already-captured WAV at seal/summarize time
  — pitch (autocorrelation/YIN), energy (RMS), pause detection (VAD gaps), a light laughter/tension
  classifier — emitted as a compact per-second envelope stored alongside the meeting; **plus the
  existing dual-stream Me/Others** gives the two threads for free. A new command returns the envelope +
  the audio-second index the FE already has for citations.
- **Render (M):** generate the `.svg` from the envelope and **write it into the vault** as an owned asset
  next to the note (owned-files atom; export path already exists). Deterministic, no model.
- **FE (Angular, M):** a `<mur-soundprint>` design-system component (signals-first) — the SVG terrain +
  playhead + click-to-audio-second (reuse the `pin_moment`/audio-anchor path) + the Pause Garden. Gate
  every audio read through `meeting_is_unlocked` (a locked meeting shows no soundprint / no playback).
- **Limits — needs a real Mac:** the prosody classifiers (laughter/tension) and far-side thread
  separation only truly validate on real captured audio on a signed build; the procedural terrain here
  is a stand-in for the *feeling*, not the real DSP. **Echo Locate** shares the audio-second seam (small
  extra: emphasis-ranked retrieval). **The Mother** and **A Letter in Five Years** are separate builds
  (a living-fermentation loop; a time-gated CK release) — parked as their own dreams.
- Lock-touching (any audio playback path) → **lock-security-reviewer** required.
