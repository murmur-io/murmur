# Dream: turning Brain Live (the "whisper") into a killer feature

We dreamed ON the shipped Realtime-Reactions feature (`src-tauri/src/brain_reactions.rs`):
today, during a recording, a local light LLM extracts `entity·predicate·object` triples from the
last ~2–3 sentences; a **deterministic** reconcile flags when the far side asserts a value that
contradicts a fact **you** recorded, and surfaces a private, on-device, ephemeral, lock-gated card.
Sibling deterministic "Proactive Recall" (`proactive.rs`) surfaces past-meeting/commitment hits.

Four divergence lenses ran in parallel (SCAMPER / cross-domain analogy raid / only-Murmur-could /
provocateur) → ~54 ideas. One ridge won **independently in all four**.

## The spark  (survivors)
1. **Receipts** *(the killer — prototyped)* — What if the whisper stops fact-checking the far side
   against **your** notes and starts fact-checking them against **their own voice-verified past
   claims**, across every meeting — then hands you a one-tap **"quote it back"** receipt, live, only
   you, on-device? Not "your data disagrees" — *"on May 3rd, in your own voice, you said the
   opposite, and here's the tape."*
2. **The Pre-Cog Brief** *(the BEFORE)* — the moment a name hits your calendar, the brain pre-loads
   that person's open contradictions, unkept promises, and their prior numbers — you walk in armed.
   Same engine, run ahead of the call instead of during.
3. **The consistency ledger** *(the accumulation / vision)* — each person quietly accrues an
   **on-device, lock-gated** consistency record from their own walk-backs. Framed neutrally
   ("consistency", "on file"), NOT the creepy "trust-score rap sheet" the provocateur pushed — that
   version is deliberately sanded down to stay shippable.

## Why these survived  (atoms + emotion)
- **Receipts** stands on **far-side capture** (botless — we own the other person's raw audio) +
  **voice** (on-device provenance: *this* voiceprint said it) + **the brain** (temporal
  `valid_from/valid_to` fact-graph per person). Otter/Granola/Fireflies lack **all three** — they
  don't botlessly hear the far side, don't run private on-device inference, don't hold a per-person
  claim history. It is a *direct evolution* of the shipped engine, not a new subsystem: the reconcile
  is identical; only the "existing facts" set changes from *your* facts → *the far-side speaker's own
  prior voice-attributed facts*. Emotion: **the receipt** — clean, quiet power in the room.
- **Pre-Cog Brief** reuses the same graph, moved earlier — cheap, and it makes Receipts feel
  inevitable rather than lucky.
- **Ledger** is the moat that compounds: every meeting makes the next one sharper, and it *cannot*
  exist off-device without becoming a liability bomb — which is exactly why only we can ship it.

## What it'd really take  (honest)
- **Backend (M):** the reconcile already exists. New work = attribute live far-side triples to a
  **speaker identity** (voiceprint) and reconcile against that *person's* prior facts, not just the
  user's. Needs a per-speaker fact scope + voiceprint match on the live far-side stream. Facts must
  carry a speaker/voiceprint key; today they're meeting-scoped. New Tauri event fields on `WhisperCard`
  (`speakerLabel`, `voiceVerified`, `sayThis`).
- **Voiceprint provenance is the load-bearing risk** — enrollment + match confidence on-device, and
  it only TRULY verifies on a **signed build on a real Mac** with live far-side capture (headless
  can't prove it). "Quote it back" text is extractive/templated (their words + yours) → stays inside
  the redaction firewall; no new egress.
- **FE (S–M):** the Receipts rail is a variant of the existing whisper card + a "say-this"
  teleprompter + the person consistency chip. All signals/IPC, no new deps.
- **Ledger persistence (M)** + **lock-security review required** (a per-person consistency record is
  new content that MUST be gated + sealed like everything else).
- Keep the darker provocateur framings (trust-score, prosody, kompromat) OUT of v1 — neutral
  "consistency / on the record" only.

## Verdict
**Receipts REJECTED (2026-07-05)** — user wants a *different* spark from the graveyard, not the
far-side-self-contradiction ridge. Prototype + journal kept as fuel. Next: re-diverge on the
alternative veins below (feel-don't-read delivery / vault-self-heals / the shield / counter-intel /
merge-conflict UI). Whichever the user picks → prototype that instead.
