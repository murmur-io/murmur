# Colors

Source: `src/design-tokens/colors.css`. The dark palette is the base `:root` ramp; the light theme,
the Paper skin and the accent palettes override it.

- **Surfaces** stack from `--surface-base` (the body) up. `--surface-raised` is the translucent
  frosted card; `--surface-overlay` is the **opaque** ground every popover, menu and `<option>`
  must use (T3).
- **Text** has four tiers. Anything set at **12px or smaller** uses `--text-tertiary` —
  `--text-muted` is below the legibility floor at that size and is for ≥13px only.
- **Accent** is the one cool colour for calm states; `--accent-text` is the accent *as text*,
  separate from `--accent-hover` (a button ground), because the two pull in opposite directions.
- **Live** is the one warm colour, reserved for recording.
