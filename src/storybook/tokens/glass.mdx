# Glass

Source: `src/design-tokens/glass.css`. Modelled on macOS Liquid Glass’s three layers (HIG
“Materials”): **highlight** (the specular rim), **shadow** (depth) and **illumination** (the
adaptive translucent fill over the aurora field). Neutral by design — the accent never fills
chrome, it only colours the active glyph or label.

`--glass-user-alpha` (0–1) is the Settings transparency slider, applied inline on `<html>` by
`GlassService`: 1 = full glass, 0 = opaque. `--shell-glass-veil` is a solid layer whose alpha is
the inverse of the slider, painted over the glass fill; `prefers-reduced-transparency` forces it
to 0.
