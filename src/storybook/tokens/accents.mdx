# Accents

Source: `src/design-tokens/accents.css`. The default purple lives in `colors.css`; picking another
accent in Settings → Appearance stamps `data-accent="<id>"` on `<html>` and these blocks re-tint
the accent ramp and its derived tokens, for dark, explicit light and System-mode light. It is
imported after `theme-light.css` on purpose, so a chosen accent wins the `--accent-soft` /
`--shadow-accent` tie. The `--accent-option-*` swatches are skin-independent — the picker has to
paint every option whichever one is active.
