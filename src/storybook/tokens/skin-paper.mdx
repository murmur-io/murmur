# Paper skin

Source: `src/design-tokens/skin-paper.css`. A **skin** is a second axis orthogonal to light/dark:
`data-theme` decides how bright the app is, `data-skin` what it is made of. *Studio* (the default)
stamps no attribute; *Paper* stamps `data-skin="paper"`.

Paper is a quiet document skin — no glass, no aurora, no blur; opaque warm surfaces, hairline
borders instead of shadows, tighter radii, the system serif for the document body and an ink-blue
accent. Every text/background pair was measured to ≥ 4.5:1. It is imported after
`theme-light.css` and before `accents.css`, so a chosen accent still wins over the skin’s default.

Switch the toolbar to **Skin → Paper** to see it.
